COST OF A WRONG ROUTING
Bot sends 22.8% of requests to the wrong team. Each such request needs 1.44 transfers on average (1.44 x Rs 305) plus one extra customer contact (Rs 260) = Rs 698.
Bot misrouting at 726 requests a month: Rs 115,716 a month, on top of the licence (Rs 26,667 a month).
91% of all transfers come from the bot's wrong routings.
